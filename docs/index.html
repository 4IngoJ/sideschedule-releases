Questions

The ones that actually come up.

Does it work with my calendar?

SideSchedule reads from Apple Calendar (EventKit), so anything Apple Calendar can see — iCloud, Google, Exchange, CalDAV — shows up in the sidebar. No extra sync, no account.

Why does it need Accessibility access?

That's the only way macOS lets an app keep other windows from sliding under the sidebar. The Dock gets its reserved strip from the system itself; a regular app has no such API, so Accessibility is the only route left. SideSchedule never reads window content — it only repositions frames.

What happens after the 14-day trial?

The sidebar stops appearing and the app opens to the activation window instead. Nothing is deleted: your notes, settings and exported .md files all stay exactly where they are, and the sidebar returns the moment you enter a key. No reinstall needed.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Your calendar events and notes stay on your Mac. The only network calls are license validation (Lemon Squeezy) and update checks (GitHub). There's no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.

Does it work on multiple monitors?

Yes. You pick which display the sidebar lives on, and it follows that screen. If you unplug the monitor, it moves to the next one automatically.
